The trilogy at a glance

A speculative trilogy spanning prehistoric survival, an ancient republic, and a synthetic future—three worlds confronting who gets to rule, own, define, or save another life.

Positioning

One moral problem, changing systems of power

The prequel asks what survival permits people to possess. The original turns that problem toward law, lineage, slavery, and institutional legitimacy. The sequel asks whether created intelligence can be recognized as a person before ownership and utility define it instead.

Current public material is deliberately premise-level. Endings, private story architecture, and unreleased development material are not part of the press surface.
