The series at a glance

A four-book speculative cycle about the forms power uses to justify defining another life—from guarded survival and institutional ownership, through synthetic personhood, into a materially abundant age shaped by measurement and comparative human value.

Positioning

A child meant to justify slavery becomes the accusation against it

At the centre of the original, an elite slaveholder manufactures a private scandal among people he owns by withholding a truth only he knows. Their child is intended as proof that domination is necessary; Yasael becomes the opposite. The surrounding books place that logic under different pressures: survival before it, created intelligence after it, then a later society where material abundance does not end the human hunger to be seen, chosen, and valued.

Current public material is deliberately premise-level. Endings, private story architecture, and unreleased development material are not part of the press surface.
