The trilogy at a glance

A speculative trilogy about the excuses power invents to justify control—from guarded survival, to a child meant to prove slavery necessary, to a future fighting over whether a created mind can be owned.

Positioning

A child meant to justify slavery becomes the accusation against it

At the centre of the original, an elite slaveholder manufactures a private scandal among people he owns by withholding a truth only he knows. Their child is intended as proof that domination is necessary; Yasael becomes the opposite. The prequel and sequel place that same logic under different pressures: survival first, then created intelligence and personhood.

Current public material is deliberately premise-level. Endings, private story architecture, and unreleased development material are not part of the press surface.
