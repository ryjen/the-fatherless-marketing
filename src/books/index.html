Entanglement of Ages

Four ages. Four forms of authority.

Across deep history and distant futures, humanity repeatedly discovers something real—strength, law, intelligence, measurement—and then risks mistaking the form for the life it represents.

Editorial status

The series remains in development.

Books I–III have beta manuscripts. Book IV, The Age of Forms, has completed its initial architecture pass and is now in representative-prose testing.

Editors, beta readers, subject-matter readers, and other contributors are welcome to register interest without posting manuscript text, spoilers, unpublished story material, raw feedback, or personal contact information.

Planned formats

The same stories, designed for different ways of reading.

The publication roadmap treats each format as a sibling edition derived from the same story authority—not as a replacement for another edition.

These are development plans, not release announcements. Availability, dates, and participating books will be announced separately when production is ready.

Planned · Core edition

Prose

The primary long-form reading edition, with reflowable ebook output and planned print editions including paperback and hardcover.

Planned · Visual companion

Illustrated prose

The prose text remains reflowable while selected artwork adds atmosphere, environments, and visual interpretation without turning the book into a fixed-layout comic.

In development · Sibling adaptation

Graphic novel

A distinct visual adaptation built from canonical page and panel sources, with planned comic-reader, fixed-layout ebook, and print-ready derivatives.

Planned · Spoken edition

Audiobook

A dedicated spoken-word edition is part of the publishing roadmap. Narration, production approach, and release timing have not yet been announced.

Film and television development is treated separately as adaptation work rather than as a book publication format.
