The trilogy has a public home

The public site now introduces Age of Embers, The Fatherless, and Neurion as one story moving across three radically different ages.

The new presentation centers the shared dramatic problem rather than explaining the trilogy through hidden symbolism: who gets to survive, who gets to rule, and who gets to be recognized as a person.

Each era now has its own visual language and cinematic hero artwork while remaining part of a single design system. The prequel moves through glacial survival, the original through monumental civic power, and the sequel through distributed synthetic life.

This update is about the public presentation of the project. It does not announce a publication or release date. Future public announcements will be posted here and available through the site feed.
