About The Fatherless

Across three ages, people with power keep finding reasons why someone else must be controlled. The stories begin where those reasons start to fail.

The trilogy

The excuse changes

Age of Embers begins with guarded survival. The Fatherless turns toward a Republic that manufactures the guilt it needs to justify ownership. Neurion carries the pattern into a future where creating intelligence becomes a new excuse to possess it.

Ryan Jennings is the creator of The Fatherless. This website contains the deliberately public edition of the project; drafts, canon development, editorial history, and unreleased story material remain private.
