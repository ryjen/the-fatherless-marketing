About The Fatherless

Three ages explore the same recurring problem: what gives one person, institution, or intelligence the right to define another life?

The trilogy

Power changes form

Age of Embers begins in prehistoric survival. The Fatherless moves the question into the laws and hierarchies of the Aurelian Republic. Neurion carries it into a future where synthetic intelligence and ownership collide.
