About Entanglement of Ages

Across four ages, people and systems keep finding reasons why someone else can be defined, owned, ranked, or controlled. The stories begin where those reasons start to fail.

Entanglement of Ages

The form changes

Age of Embers begins with guarded survival. The Fatherless turns toward a Republic that manufactures the guilt it needs to justify ownership. Neurion carries the pattern into a future where creating intelligence becomes a new excuse to possess it. The Age of Forms moves into material abundance, where useful measurements can begin standing in for the people they only partially describe.
