# Original visual direction — Institutional Eclipse

This document defines the public-safe maintenance direction for the original *The Fatherless* visual identity. Canon-sensitive prompts and interpretation remain in the private author repository.

Tracked by #64. The base theme reset landed in PR #17.

## Intent

The original should feel like a **dark civic and institutional drama**, not a sepia historical page and not a devotional/Biblical poster.

The public visual language should emphasize:

- monumental civic scale;
- hierarchy and controlled access;
- crowds and public pressure;
- cold material surfaces;
- human vulnerability within institutional geometry;
- environmental/celestial unease without assigning religious meaning.

## Palette

Primary direction:

- graphite and blue-black backgrounds;
- cold stone / desaturated steel neutrals;
- oxidized teal / verdigris primary accent;
- restrained muted-wine secondary atmosphere;
- off-white text with cool rather than parchment warmth.

Avoid brown, sepia, parchment beige, bronze, amber, or antique gold as dominant surfaces or grading.

## Typography

The original may use the shared body-reading system, but display and interface treatment should read as contemporary editorial/institutional rather than faux-antique.

Do not rely on historical-looking display fonts, inscription effects, distressed lettering, or manuscript styling to communicate the era.

## Image composition

Prefer:

- civic halls, arches, galleries, roads, thresholds, stairs, records, barriers, and crowds;
- asymmetry and layered depth;
- people embedded within a social scene rather than isolated as icons;
- visible scale differences between architecture/institutions and individual people;
- controlled negative space for hero copy;
- dim or anomalous natural light used as atmosphere.

Avoid:

- halos or radiant backlight around a person;
- saint-like portraiture;
- Madonna-and-child composition;
- central lone figures visually authenticated by the sky;
- dominant cross-like framing;
- crowns, candles, olive branches, broken chains, or similar props used as generic moral/religious shorthand;
- parchment maps or antique still lifes as the default identity;
- familiar Bible-film/Renaissance-painting composition.

## Hero contract

The homepage/original hero should:

1. read as institutional/civic before religious;
2. preserve a clear text-safe region;
3. work at representative mobile and desktop crops;
4. remain understandable without the image;
5. avoid making one person look supernaturally selected;
6. support the graphite/blue-black/teal theme without requiring brown or gold overlays.

Hero copy must retain a bounded high-contrast surface or equivalent guaranteed contrast treatment. Do not depend on image grading alone for readability.

## Supporting media

Add supporting images only when they have a concrete reader, press, or release use. Prefer environmental/editorial scenes over a gallery of conventional character posters.

Suitable public-safe subjects include:

- civic architecture and public gathering;
- administrative/record environments;
- roads, tribunals, galleries, or controlled thresholds;
- crowds and social scale;
- observational character scenes with contextual surroundings.

## Asset governance

Every published raster asset must follow the existing media/publication rules:

- known rights/provenance status;
- AI-generation/commission/stock/public-domain classification as applicable;
- metadata review/stripping;
- responsive AVIF/WebP derivatives with fallback;
- intrinsic dimensions;
- public-safe alt text describing visible content only;
- public-manifest registration before deployment;
- mobile/desktop crop and text-contrast review.

## Trilogy separation

This direction applies to the original and original-default public surfaces only.

- **Prequel:** retains its glacial/ember/elemental identity.
- **Original:** graphite, cold civic stone, oxidized teal, institutional pressure.
- **Sequel:** retains its luminous/networked future identity.

Do not make the three eras visually uniform merely to simplify CSS or image grading.

## Validation

Before accepting an original visual change:

- [ ] no brown/sepia/parchment regression;
- [ ] no devotional/Biblical-poster shorthand;
- [ ] no unreadable hero copy at representative crops;
- [ ] no prequel/sequel palette regression;
- [ ] alt text describes only visible content;
- [ ] provenance/manifest metadata is complete;
- [ ] visual-regression baselines in #62 are refreshed only after the intended design is stable.
