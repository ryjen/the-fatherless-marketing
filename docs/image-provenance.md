# Hero image provenance

The production hero artwork is public-native raster material derived from the project-generated cinematic site concept preserved at `docs/reference/site-concept-v1.webp`. No stock photography, private authoring files, manuscript imagery, logos, or third-party source assets are incorporated.

Only the image-only scene regions were promoted to production. Illustrative UI, labels, buttons, and mockup copy were excluded before cropping. Each production image was resized to `640 × 360`, encoded as WebP, and exported without EXIF or XMP metadata.

## Production decision

These WebPs replace the earlier geometric SVG placeholders. The raster scenes better match the approved cinematic direction while remaining small enough that a single `640 × 360` encode per era is appropriate for the current presentation. The intrinsic dimensions are declared in markup, card images remain lazy-loaded, and the primary homepage/title hero retains eager priority behavior.

The preserved full-page concept remains a **design reference**, not a deployable source asset. Production promotion applies only to the reviewed scene crops listed above; no illustrative mockup text or interface treatment becomes approved story content by association.

## Review

The three production images were reviewed against the public image-direction brief:

- no devotional or Christianity-coded composition;
- no sepia/parchment historical treatment;
- no sacred central figure, halo, cross framing, or mother-and-child iconography;
- no generic caveman portrait or ritual-fire poster;
- no glowing robot saviour, Matrix code, or single-central-AI composition;
- no baked-in text, logos, private identifiers, or hidden story mechanics;
- descriptive `alt` text remains supplied by page markup rather than embedded in the image;
- WebP byte inspection reports no EXIF or XMP metadata markers.
