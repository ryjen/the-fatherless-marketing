# Hero image provenance

The production hero artwork is public-native raster material derived from the project-generated cinematic site concept preserved at `docs/reference/site-concept-v1.webp`. No stock photography, private authoring files, manuscript imagery, logos, or third-party source assets are incorporated.

Only the image-only scene regions were promoted. Illustrative UI, labels, buttons, and mockup copy were excluded before cropping. The three governed masters are `1440 × 810` WebPs under `staging/media-masters/`, exported without EXIF or XMP metadata.

## Responsive production

The masters are not copied directly into deployable `src/`. During `scripts/build.sh`, pinned Sharp generates an exact responsive matrix into `dist/media/heroes/`:

- AVIF at 480, 960, and 1440 pixels wide;
- WebP at 480, 960, and 1440 pixels wide;
- 16:9 intrinsic dimensions at every size;
- AVIF quality 58 / effort 5 and WebP quality 76 / effort 5.

Every generated derivative path, dimension, format, and SHA-256 is recorded in `public-manifest.json`. The build fails if generated bytes differ from the approved checksum, and publication validation rejects unexpected output or embedded EXIF/XMP metadata.

Page markup uses `<picture>` with AVIF and WebP `srcset` values. Full-width heroes use `sizes="100vw"`; trilogy cards use `(max-width: 64rem) 100vw, 33vw`. The 1440px WebP is the explicit fallback, primary heroes retain eager priority behavior, and card images remain lazy-loaded.

These raster scenes replace the earlier geometric SVG placeholders. The preserved full-page concept remains a **design reference**, not a deployable source asset; no illustrative mockup text or interface treatment becomes approved story content by association.

## Review

The three production images were reviewed against the public image-direction brief:

- no devotional or Christianity-coded composition;
- no sepia/parchment historical treatment;
- no sacred central figure, halo, cross framing, or mother-and-child iconography;
- no generic caveman portrait or ritual-fire poster;
- no glowing robot saviour, Matrix code, or single-central-AI composition;
- no baked-in text, logos, private identifiers, or hidden story mechanics;
- descriptive `alt` text is supplied by page markup and describes only visible scene content;
- master and generated derivative byte checks reject embedded EXIF or XMP metadata.
